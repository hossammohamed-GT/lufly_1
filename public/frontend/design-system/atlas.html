14 / Accessibility

Premium means inclusive.

Accessibility is part of the finish: visible, testable, and considered from the first component.

Contrast

Text and actions target WCAG AA contrast.

Focus

Primary teal focus ring remains visible on every surface.

Targets

Interactive controls use a minimum 44px touch area.

Language

Labels and states stay clear, short, and semantic.

Reduced motion: all non-essential animations stop when the user preference is enabled.
